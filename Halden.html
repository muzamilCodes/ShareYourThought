§ 01 — Method

A timer that trusts you to know when to stop.

Written
Nov. 2025
— Oslo

Most focus tools want to gamify your attention. They count streaks, hand out badges, rank you against strangers.

Halden does none of that. It is a single dark screen, a serif numeral, and the quiet promise that when the ninety minutes end, nothing will interrupt you.

Not a nudge. Not a chime. Not a “you're on a 7-day streak!” The reward for deep work is the deep work.

§ 02 — Instruments

Three things it does. Nothing else.

01Ambient

Room tone.

Six recorded rooms — a Reykjavík library, a rainy Kyoto attic, a small chapel at dawn. Loops perfectly. Never louder than a page turning.

02Log

An honest ledger.

A one-line log of what you did each session, kept in a plain text file you own. No charts. No graphs pretending to be insight.

Mon · book ch.41h 22m
Tue · book ch.51h 34m

03Silence

System-wide quiet.

Halden takes over Do Not Disturb, hides your dock, mutes Slack — and puts it all back exactly as it was when the session ends.
